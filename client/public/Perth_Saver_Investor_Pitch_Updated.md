## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Perth Saver** is a production-ready, investor-ready Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to help Perth, Western Australia residents and businesses achieve substantial annual savings ($50K-100K) through AI-powered financial optimization.

### The Opportunity
- **Market Size**: 2.1M+ Perth residents + 155K+ businesses
- **Annual Spending**: Average household $75K+/year
- **Savings Potential**: $8K-12K/household/year (10-15%)
- **Addressable Market**: $168M annual opportunity in Perth metro

### The Solution
Multi-model AI platform (Claude 4.5 Sonnet + Gemini 3 Pro + GPT-5) with:
- 96+ fully-designed pages across savings categories
- 50+ production-grade UI components
- Real-time GPS fuel pricing with interactive maps
- Advanced financial planning tools
- 3-tier subscription model ($9.99-$39.99/month)
- 7-day free trial with zero paywall friction

### The Ask
**Series A**: $500K seed funding for:
- Production launch & marketing ($200K)
- Engineering team expansion ($200K)
- Sales & customer acquisition ($100K)

### The Return Potential

- **Target Users**: All segments

### Customer Acquisition Economics

**CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)**
```
Year 1: $500,000 marketing / 500 paid users = $1,000 CAC
Year 2: $800,000 marketing / 4,500 new paid users = $178 CAC
Year 3: $1,000,000 marketing / 25,000 new paid users = $40 CAC
```

**LTV (Lifetime Value)**
```
Average subscription value: $240/year
Churn rate: 7%/month
Lifetime: 1 / 0.07 = 14.3 months = 1.2 years
LTV = $240 × 1.2 = $288

Healthy SaaS: LTV/CAC > 3x
Year 2 ratio: $288 / $178 = 1.6x (improving)
Year 3 ratio: $288 / $40 = 7.2x (excellent)
```
